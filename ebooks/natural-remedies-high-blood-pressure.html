About this guide

What’s inside this guide.

High blood pressure is famously quiet — it rarely announces itself, which is exactly what makes it worth taking seriously. This guide focuses on the three levers with the strongest track record behind them: what you eat, how you move, and how you handle stress. It explains the DASH approach in practical terms and turns it into changes you can actually keep up.

The DASH diet, made practical

What the DASH eating pattern actually asks of you, translated into real meals, shopping decisions, and swaps rather than abstract rules.

Movement that helps

The kind and amount of activity most associated with healthier blood pressure, and how to build up to it without overdoing it.

Stress reduction

Why stress and sleep belong in a blood pressure conversation, and simple techniques you can use in an ordinary day.

Heart-healthy habits

The everyday factors — sodium, alcohol, weight, and consistency — that quietly move the number in one direction or the other.
